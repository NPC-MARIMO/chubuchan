The City Montessori School (India) was awarded the 2002 UNESCO Prize for Peace Education

City Montessori School, Lucknow, was awarded the UNESCO Prize for Peace Education in 2002, in recognition of its efforts to promote the universal values of education for peace and tolerance, at a time when such values were increasingly being challenged.

City Montessori School, Lucknow, was listed in the 2023 Guinness Book of World Records as the world's largest school by enrolment, with 61,345 pupils enrolled on 10 August 2023 for the 2023–2024 academic session.

Our Philosphy

Founded in 1959 with just five children and a borrowed capital of ₹300 by Dr Jagdish Gandhi and Dr Bharti Gandhi, City Montessori School (CMS) began with the vision of inspiring generations of children to become socially motivated and conscious citizens of the world.

Inspired by the ideals of Mahatma Gandhi and Sant Vinoba Bhave, the school embraced the spirit of peace and global unity through the motto “Jai Jagat” (Hail the World), in contrast to the post-Independence greeting “Jai Hind” (Hail India).

At CMS, we believe that 21st-century education must be ‘broader’ and ‘bolder’. It must nurture a ‘good human being’ with values and a broad range of capabilities. Therefore, CMS education recognises the three fundamental realities of human life—Material, Human, and Divine. It is built upon four building blocks: Universal Values, Global Understanding, Service to the World, and Excellence in All Things.

CMS Cambridge Section

At CMS Cambridge, we are committed to providing an exceptional educational experience that prepares students for success in a globalised world. Cambridge Assessment International Education (CAIE) is a part of the University of Cambridge, one of the world’s top universities, which is trusted for excellence in education. It offers world-renowned IGCSE and A Level programmes.

The IGCSE (International General Certificate of Secondary Education) qualification is recognised by the Council of Boards of School Education (COBSE). It is considered equivalent to any Class X board examination in India. The A Level qualification is recognised by the Association of Indian Universities (AIU) as equivalent to Class XII qualifications offered by the Indian boards.
